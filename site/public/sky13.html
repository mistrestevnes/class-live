The Four Elements

For about 2,000 years, people believed everything below the Moon was made of four elements, each a mix of two qualities.

ElementQualitiesNatural place
Earthcold and drylowest, at the center
Watercold and weton top of earth
Airhot and weton top of water
Firehot and dryhighest, just below the Moon

Each element moves in a straight line toward its natural place, then stops. Earth and water move down, toward the center. Air and fire move up, away from it.

Above the Moon, the heavens are made of a fifth element. It never changes and moves in circles forever.
